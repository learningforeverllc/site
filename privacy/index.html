Sproutle Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026 · Sproutle is made by Learning Forever LLC, California, USA

Sproutle is a virtual pet that grows from your steps and sleep. This page explains what the app reads, what leaves your phone, who can see it, and how to delete it.

Apple Health

With your permission, Sproutle reads your step count and sleep from Apple Health. It never writes to Apple Health.

  • Health data is used only to feed and rest your pet and to show your own stats.
  • Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared except as described under Friends.
  • Your sleep never leaves your phone. It is not in your backup and friends never see it.

What is stored on our server

Sproutle's server is hosted by Supabase. It stores:

  • An account ID, made automatically the first time you open the app. It is anonymous unless you choose to add an email or Sign in with Apple.
  • Your email address, only if you save your account with it. It is used to send sign-in codes and nothing else.
  • Your friend code, your pet's name and what friends see: your pet's look, mood, stage and bars.
  • Your step counts (today, your 7-day average and your step level), only if you choose to show them to friends. This is off until you turn it on.
  • A private backup of your pet (its bars, food, petals, items and recent daily step counts), so you can bring it back on a new phone. Only you can read it.
  • Your friend list, gifts, nudges and hangouts.
  • People you block and reports you send, so we can keep blocked people away from you and review reports.

Friends

People you accept as friends can see your pet, its name, mood and stage, and your step counts only if you allow it. They never see your sleep. You can unfriend, block or report anyone from the Friends tab.

Other services

  • Apple Push Notification service and Expo deliver notifications (friend invites, nudges, gifts and, if you turn them on, new items in the Shop). They receive a device token and the notification text, which can include a friend's or pet's name. Notifications never include your steps or sleep.
  • Expo (EAS Update) delivers app updates. It sees your device's app version, not your Health data.

No ads, no tracking

Sproutle shows no ads, uses no ad networks and does not track you across other apps or websites. We do not sell any data.

Emails you send us

"Send a note to the developer" and "Report" open your own Mail app. If you send it, we receive your message, the app version, your pet's name, stage and color, and for a report the friend code you reported.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete my account. Seven days later your account, friend list, gifts, blocks and pet backup are permanently removed from our server and the pet on your phone is erased; the app tells you when it is done. You can cancel any time before then. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone right away. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Children

Sproutle is meant for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Learning Forever LLC · apps@learningforever.net. In the app: Settings → Send a note to the developer. We review reports within 24 hours.
